CV, Cover Letter & Job Description Matching Guide

How to align your application materials with job requirements

Understanding the Job Description (JD)

  • Key Responsibilities: Identify 3-5 core functions of the role
  • Required Skills: List technical and soft skills mentioned
  • Qualifications: Note education, experience, and certifications
  • Compensation & Benefits: Understand what's being offered
  • Application Requirements: Check all required documents

Building Your CV (Curriculum Vitae)

  • Tailor to JD: Customize for each position you apply for
  • Action Verbs: Use strong verbs: Led, Implemented, Managed, Analyzed
  • Quantify Achievements: Use numbers - "Managed team of 15" vs "Managed team"
  • Relevant Experience: Prioritize roles matching job requirements
  • Format: One page for entry-level, 2 pages max for experienced
  • Keywords: Match key terms from the job description

Writing Your Cover Letter

  • Address Specifically: Use hiring manager's name if possible
  • Show Alignment: Map your skills to their needs
  • Tell Your Story: Explain why you're interested in THIS role
  • Demonstrate Knowledge: Show research about the organization
  • Keep It Concise: 3-4 paragraphs, under one page
  • Call to Action: End with enthusiasm to discuss further

CV/CL/JD Matching Checklist

  • [ ] All JD requirements addressed in CV/CL?
  • [ ] Used relevant keywords from JD?
  • [ ] Quantified achievements and results?
  • [ ] Showed understanding of organization?
  • [ ] Demonstrated passion for the sector?
  • [ ] No typos or grammatical errors?
  • [ ] PDF saved with professional filename?
  • [ ] Followed application instructions exactly?
