The Problem
What students are experiencing right now
High stress · Low clarity

Placement season should feel structured and predictable, but most students are navigating it in the dark – chasing messages, guessing timelines, and comparing themselves silently.

  • ⚠️ Unclear timelines – no single view of what is happening when.
  • 📌 Scattered info – updates spread across WhatsApp, email, and folders.
  • ⚖️ Unequal support – some students get 1-1 guidance, others feel lost.
  • 💭 High stress – constant pressure, comparison, and fear of falling behind.
Goal: turn a stressful, opaque process into a calm, transparent system.
Commitment 1
✓ Live Master Calendar

A single calendar where every important placement event lives – always up to date, always in one place.

  • • See all deadlines, recruiter sessions, and internal tasks in one view.
  • • Filter by track (Tier-1, social sector, fellowships, etc.).
  • • Plan calmly instead of reacting at the last minute.
"Know exactly what's happening, when." Source of truth
Commitment 2
✓ 4 Support Tracks

Structured support for very different placement journeys – instead of one generic "prep".

  • • Tier-1 prep: high-rigour practice for competitive roles.
  • • Diverse careers: social sector, CSR, development, and fellowships.
  • • Skill-building: CV/CL labs, mock interviews, and peer review circles.
  • • Personal support: doubt-clearing, small groups, and 1-1 check-ins.
"Support that matches where you are." 4-track model
Commitment 3
✓ Weekly Updates

A once-a-week snapshot of everything that actually matters – no more hunting for scattered messages.

  • • New openings, deadlines, and shortlists in one concise update.
  • • Clear "this week focus" for each support track.
  • • Linked back to the master calendar and resources.
"One place to catch up, once a week." Update rhythm
Commitment 4
✓ Mental Health Support

Honest conversations and safe spaces around anxiety, rejection, and non-linear journeys.

  • • Real Talk circles on fear, comparison, and uncertainty.
  • • Check-ins after major results, not just "success stories".
  • • We celebrate all outcomes – jobs, higher studies, fellowships, experiments, and pauses.
"You are not your offer letter." Real talk
